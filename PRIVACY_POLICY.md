# Privacy Policy — ToolBox

_Last updated: 24 October 2026. Developer: Pushpendra Tech (business@pushpendra.tech)._
> Hosting note: publish this file as a public web page (e.g. https://pushpendra.tech/toolbox-privacy/) and use that URL in Play Console. A local file alone is not a public URL.

## What the app does
ToolBox provides 102 file and productivity tools that run **entirely on your device**.

## Data we collect
**None.** The app has no server, no user accounts, and no analytics. We cannot see, receive, or store your files or personal information.

## Your files
PDFs, images, videos and audio you open are processed locally on your phone. They are **never uploaded** by the app. Temporary working copies are kept in the app's private cache and outputs are saved only where you choose (via Android's file picker / share sheet).

## Stored on your device only
The app saves your favorite tools and recently used tools (tool names only — no file data) in on-device storage so those sections work. Uninstalling the app removes this.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device information including the Android Advertising ID, IP address, and ad interaction data, as described in Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and "How Google uses data from apps that use its services". Where required (EEA/UK), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form, and you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. **Your documents and media are never shared with the advertising SDK.**

## Permissions
The app requests Internet + network-state access **only for ads**. Files are accessed only through Android's system file picker, for the exact files you select. No contacts, location, camera, microphone, or broad storage access is requested.

## Children's privacy
The app is a general utility, not directed at children under 13, and collects no personal data as described above.
