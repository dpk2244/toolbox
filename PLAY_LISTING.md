**Full description (rewritten 2026-10-24 for the 102-tool v7 build — supersedes the old 59-tool text):**
One app, 102 everyday tools — and every file stays on your phone. No sign-up, no uploads, no server. Almost everything works 100% offline.

📄 PDF (35): Compress, Merge, Split, Compress to Target KB, Unlock (with known password), JPG to PDF, PDF to Images, Text to PDF, PDF to Text, Scanned PDF to Text (OCR), PDF to Word, Rotate, Reorder, Delete / Extract Pages, Extract Images, Watermark, Page Numbers, Protect with Password, Header & Footer, Add Image to PDF, Sign PDF (visual stamp), Crop, Resize Pages (A4/Letter/A5), Grayscale, N-Up, Flatten, Repair, Reverse Order, Insert Blank Page, Split by Size, Fill Form, plus PDF Info & Metadata tools.
🤖 AI (7, on-device): Background Remover, OCR Text Scanner (English + Hindi), Face Blur, Photo Labeler, QR & Barcode Scanner, Offline Translator, Voice to Text.
🖼️ Image (18): Compressor, Compress to Exact KB, Batch Compressor, Resizer, Cropper, Rotator, Flipper, JPG↔PNG, WebP Converter, Collage Maker, Photo Filters, Photo Watermark, EXIF Viewer & Remover, Image to PDF, Passport Photo, Color Picker.
🎬 Video (11): Compressor, Trimmer, Video to Audio (M4A), Video to GIF, Frame Extractor, Mute, Resolution Changer, Size Estimator, Speed Changer, Rotate & Flip, Crop (9:16 / 1:1 / 16:9 for Reels & Shorts).
🎵 Audio (9): Trimmer, Merger, Converter (WAV/M4A), Volume, Speed, Format Inspector, Voice Recorder, Compressor, Fade In/Out.
✍️ Text (12): Word & Character Counter, Case Converter, Remove Duplicate Lines, Remove Extra Spaces, Reverser, Slug Maker, Find & Replace, Password Generator, QR & Barcode Generator, Text Encrypt/Decrypt, Lorem Ipsum.
🧮 Calculators (10): Percentage, GST (India), EMI, Discount, Age, Unit Converter, BMI, SIP, Tip, Date Difference.

Honest by design: if a file can't be made smaller, the app tells you instead of pretending. Audio extraction saves as M4A because Android has no MP3 encoder — we never fake a format.
